A SMALL GUIDE TO BIG QUESTIONS

Make every rule click.

A switch is either ON or OFF. Each rule contains three conditions connected by OR. At least one must match. The rules are connected by AND: they must all pass.

A ON ✓∨B OFF ×∨C ON ×→ Rule passes

Your lab, your pace

  • Tap table headers to toggle switches; tap conditions to group identical literals.
  • Find solution starts a fresh DPLL search with undecided switches. Decisions explore OFF first, unit propagation makes forced moves, and conflicts trigger backtracking.
  • Check solution visits every rule without changing any switch.
  • Both walkthroughs advance only when you press →. ← restores the earlier assignment and explanation. Stop restores your starting settings during search; at a solved final step it keeps the solution.
  • Drag network nodes, drag empty space to rotate, and pinch or scroll to zoom. Use Shift + drag to pan. Node fills show ON/OFF requirements; their colored outlines identify rules.
  • Import or export standard DIMACS CNF to study your own formulas. Generated formulas are always satisfiable; imported ones may be unsatisfiable.

Why P / NP?

3-SAT is NP-complete. Checking an assignment is quick; finding one can involve many branches. This is a tool for experiments and intuition. The DPLL solver is an exponential-time search in the worst case, not a proof that P = NP or P ≠ NP. Success on generated instances does not establish a general complexity result.

Everything runs in your browser. Your current problem is saved on this device; shared links include the formula and switch settings.
